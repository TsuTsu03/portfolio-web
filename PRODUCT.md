# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Two primary audiences:
1. **Hiring managers & recruiters** evaluating candidates for senior full-stack or AI engineering roles — they arrive skeptical, scan fast, and need evidence of depth and shipped production work.
2. **Business owners & startup founders** looking for a trusted developer to build or scale their SaaS — they need to trust competence before they reach out.

Both audiences arrive on a single portfolio page and make a decision within the first 10 seconds.

## Product Purpose

Den Jansen Flores's personal portfolio — the digital front door to a career as a Senior Full-Stack Developer and Agentic AI Engineer. Success means a potential client or hiring manager contacts Den or adds him to a shortlist. The portfolio must prove seniority and AI capability through real shipped work, not claims.

## Brand Personality

Bold, precise, technically confident.

The portfolio should feel like the work of someone who has shipped production systems — not a showcase, not a résumé website. Every design choice should communicate "I understand engineering at depth" without shouting it.

## Anti-references

- **Corporate / sterile resume sites** — bland, forgettable, reads as junior
- **Bootcamp grad showcases** — generic purple gradient SaaS templates with glassmorphism cards everywhere
- **Over-animated personal brand** — particle effects, excessive scroll tricks, distracting gimmicks

## Design Principles

1. **Ship first, polish second** — every section should communicate shipped, real, production-grade work. Screenshots over mockups. Live links over "coming soon".
2. **Seniority is shown, not told** — architectural decisions, business context, and real outcomes in project descriptions. Never just "built a web app".
3. **Dark and deliberate** — dark palette is committed, not trendy. Every color and contrast choice should be intentional.
4. **AI identity is native, not bolted on** — Agentic AI development appears throughout the portfolio naturally, not as a badge added at the end.
5. **Copy converts** — the portfolio is sales material. Every section heading and description should move a visitor toward contact.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA as minimum for body text contrast (≥ 4.5:1)
- Prefers-reduced-motion support for all animations
- Semantic HTML with proper heading hierarchy
- All interactive elements keyboard-accessible
